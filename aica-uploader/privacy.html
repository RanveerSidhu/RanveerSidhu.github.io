Privacy Policy – aica-uploader

Last updated: 2026-11-11

aica-uploader is a personal tool that uploads videos to the developer's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API. It is used only by its developer, Ranveer Singh Sidhu.

Data accessed

With the account owner's consent (Google OAuth), the tool uses the YouTube scope to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags and publish times, and read back their status. It does not access any other Google data.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored only on the developer's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's APIs, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services and is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
