Privacy Policy - Sock Sort: Laundry Day

Last updated: 2026-10-08

Sock Sort: Laundry Day ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Publisher: Ranveer Singh Sidhu - Contact: dev.ranveersinghsidhu@gmail.com - Policy URL: https://ranveersidhu.github.io/socksort/privacy.html

The short version

  • The game has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. Your progress is stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which collects and uses some device data to serve ads. In regions where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time (Settings > Privacy & ad choices).
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Information that stays on your device

Level progress, coins, boosters, laundromat renovations, daily-challenge and streak data, and your sound/music/vibration settings are saved in a local file in the game's private storage. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it (and Android backup is disabled for the game).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded and interstitial video ads. When an ad is requested, Google and its partners may collect and process:

  • your Android advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app name/version, time zone, battery/volume state where relevant),
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks, ad performance and diagnostics).

This is used to deliver ads, limit repeat ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and - if you consented where required - to personalise ads. We do not receive this data and do not combine it with anything else. How Google uses it: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions that require it, the game shows Google's consent form (UMP) on first launch. You can reopen it from Settings > Privacy & ad choices.
  • Ad personalisation: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • No ads: the Remove Ads purchase removes forced (interstitial) ads. Rewarded ads are always optional.
  • The game does not tag ad requests as child-directed because it is not directed at children; ad content is limited to the "Teen" maximum rating.

Ratings and reviews (Google Play)

Occasionally, after you finish a level, the game may ask Google Play to show its standard rating/review sheet. That sheet is provided and handled entirely by Google Play; the game does not receive your rating or review, does not collect anything for it, and does not reward or require it.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases ("Remove Ads", booster packs) are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment information under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. The game only receives confirmation that a purchase succeeded (product id and a purchase token), which it stores locally to avoid granting the same purchase twice and to restore it if you reinstall.

Optional services (not in the current release)

If a later version turns on Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) it will collect anonymous gameplay events (for example which level was played or won, which booster was used), crash reports, and a random installation identifier, processed by Google, to fix crashes and tune difficulty and ads. If a later version offers Google Play Games sign-in, your Play Games player ID and a cloud copy of your game progress are stored by Google, only if you choose to sign in. This policy will be updated before either is released.

Other data

  • We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • The game requests these Android permissions: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (to load ads and purchases), VIBRATE (haptics), WAKE_LOCK (background ad tasks), POST_NOTIFICATIONS and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (optional daily reminders, which you opt in to), the Google Play billing permission, and the advertising-ID permission used by AdMob.
  • The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of its own.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and your rights

We hold no personal data about you on our systems, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data held by Google (AdMob, Google Play), use the controls in your Google Account: https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights of access, correction, deletion and objection regarding data processed by Google; Google is the controller for that data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new "last updated" date.
