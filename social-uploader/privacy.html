Privacy Policy – Social Uploader

Last updated: 2026-11-17

Social Uploader is a private, owner-operated upload tool used only by Ranveer Sidhu to upload and schedule videos to his own YouTube channels (currently AI Code Autopsy and Snipora). It has no third-party users.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent (Google OAuth), the tool uses the youtube.upload scope to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and publish times, and the youtube.readonly scope to read back their status. It does not access any other Google data.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored only locally on the owner's own PC. No data is sent to any server other than Google's APIs, and no data is sold or shared with third parties.

YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services and is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
