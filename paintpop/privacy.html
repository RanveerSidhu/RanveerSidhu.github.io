Privacy Policy - Paint Pop: Pixel Fill

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Paint Pop: Pixel Fill ("the game") is a pixel-art puzzle game for Android. It is a general-audience, family-friendly game with no violence or blood, and it is not directed at children. This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Publisher: Ranveer Singh Sidhu - Contact: dev.ranveersinghsidhu@gmail.com - Policy URL: https://ranveersidhu.github.io/paintpop/privacy.html

The short version

No accounts, no sign-in, no servers of ours. Your progress is stored only on your device. The game uses anonymous gameplay analytics (GameAnalytics) and video ads (Google AdMob) and one optional in-app purchase (Google Play). We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

Information that stays on your device

Level progress, collected pictures, daily-picture streak, whether Remove Ads was purchased, and your music / sound / vibration settings are saved in a local file in the game's private storage. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising: Google AdMob

The game shows optional rewarded videos (watch a short video for extra bench slots) and short interstitial ads between levels through Google AdMob. The interstitials are removed by the one-time Remove Ads purchase. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may process your Android advertising ID, device information (model, operating system version, language, screen and network details), IP address and approximate location, app information and ad interactions, and, where you consented (if required), use them to personalise ads. Ad content is limited to the "G" (general audiences) rating. Where the law requires it you are asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time (Settings > Ad privacy choices). You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalisation in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. Google's policies: How Google uses data in advertising and Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics: GameAnalytics

To learn which levels are too hard or too easy, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics Ltd.): session start, tutorial completed, level started / completed / failed, retries, leaving a level, and whether an optional rewarded video was started or finished. Each event carries only the level number, number of attempts, outcome, number of taps, bench slots and, for ads, which feature the ad was for. The SDK also assigns a randomly generated anonymous user ID and records basic technical information (device model, operating system and app version, approximate country derived from the IP address). This does not identify you personally and is not linked to an account. GameAnalytics' policy: GameAnalytics Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The one-time in-app purchase "Remove Ads" is processed by Google Play under Google's own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. The game only receives confirmation that a purchase succeeded (product id and purchase token) and never sees your payment details.

Other data

Android permissions used: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (ads, analytics and purchases), VIBRATE (haptics), WAKE_LOCK (background ad tasks), the Google Play billing permission and the advertising-ID permission used by AdMob. The game contains no links that take you out of the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. Anonymous analytics are kept by GameAnalytics according to its policy. For data held by Google (AdMob, Google Play) use the controls in your Google Account. To request deletion of analytics data linked to your anonymous ID, write to the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new "last updated" date.
