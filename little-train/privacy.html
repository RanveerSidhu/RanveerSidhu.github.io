Privacy Policy - Choo Choo Tracks

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Choo Choo Tracks ("the game") is a railway path puzzle game for Android made for children and families. This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Publisher: Ranveer Singh Sidhu - Contact: dev.ranveersinghsidhu@gmail.com - Policy URL: https://ranveersidhu.github.io/little-train/privacy.html

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no servers of ours. Progress is stored only on the device.
  • The game collects no personal information from anyone. It has no links that lead out of the game.
  • Ads are optional rewarded videos from Google AdMob, requested as child-directed, limited to the "G" content rating and not personalised. The game does not use the advertising ID.
  • The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Google Play and sits behind a parent gate.

Information that stays on the device

Level progress, stars, unlocked worlds and items, the player's choices, daily-challenge and streak data, whether Remove Ads was purchased, and the time of the last free hint are saved in a local file in the game's private storage. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show optional rewarded ads, only when a player chooses to watch one (for example to get a hint or open something early). There are no banner ads and no full-screen ads that interrupt play. Ads are requested as child-directed and for users under the age of consent, with a maximum content rating of G and as non-personalised. To deliver and measure ads, Google AdMob may process device and app information, the IP address and technical data. We do not receive this data. See how Google uses ad data and Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Consent: where the law requires it, the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). It can be reopened from the settings screen, which is protected by the parent gate.
  • Advertising ID: the released app does not request the advertising-ID permission.
  • Remove Ads: hints and early unlocks become free, so no ad is needed.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional one-time purchase "Remove Ads" is processed by Google Play under Google's own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. It is shown only behind a parent gate (a simple sum a young child cannot yet solve). The game only receives confirmation that a purchase succeeded (product id and a purchase token) so it can unlock the purchase and restore it if the game is reinstalled. We never see payment details.

Other data and permissions

  • We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.
  • Android permissions in the released app: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (ads and purchases), VIBRATE (haptics), WAKE_LOCK (background ad tasks) and the Google Play billing permission.
  • The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of its own.

Children

The game is designed to be safe for children: no accounts, no chat, no social features, no links out of the game, child-directed G-rated non-personalised ads only, and purchases behind a parent gate. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and your rights

We hold no personal data about you on our systems, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data held by Google (AdMob, Google Play), use the controls in your Google Account: https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or COPPA) parents and users may have rights regarding data processed by Google; Google is the controller for that data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new "last updated" date.
