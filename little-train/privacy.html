Choo Choo Tracks – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Choo Choo Tracks is developed by Ranveer Singh Sidhu. The game is made for children and families. The developer does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Game data

Level progress, stars, unlocked trains and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

The app can show optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, only when a player chooses to watch one (for example to get a hint). There are no banner or full-screen interrupting ads. Ads are requested as child-directed, with a maximum content rating of G and as non-personalized. Google AdMob may process device information such as the IP address and technical data to deliver and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses ad data.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is protected by a parent gate and processed by Google Play. The developer never receives payment details.

Children

The app is designed to be safe for children. It has no chat, no social features, no links out of the game and collects no personal information from anyone.
