Privacy Policy - Arrow Escape

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Arrow Escape ("the game") is a tile puzzle game for Android suitable for all ages. This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Publisher: Ranveer Singh Sidhu - Contact: dev.ranveersinghsidhu@gmail.com - Policy URL: https://ranveersidhu.github.io/arrowescape/privacy.html

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. Your progress is stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads are tagged as child-directed, limited to the "G" content rating, and not personalised. In regions where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown first and can be reopened from Settings.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • The game contains no links that take you out of the game.

Information that stays on your device

Level progress, stars, hints, daily-challenge and streak data, and your sound/haptics/vibration settings are saved in a local file in the game's private storage. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows only optional rewarded ads (watch a short video to get a hint or an extra heart). It shows no pop-up (interstitial) or banner ads. To serve and measure ads, Google may process device and app information, your IP address and ad interactions. Because the game is child-directed, personalised advertising and interest-based targeting are turned off. We do not receive this data. See Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Remove Ads makes hints and hearts free, so you never need to watch an ad. Rewarded ads are always optional.
  • The game does not request the advertising ID permission.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases ("Remove Ads", "Hint Pack") are processed by Google Play under Google's own policy. The game only receives confirmation that a purchase succeeded (product id and a purchase token), which it stores locally to avoid granting the same purchase twice and to restore it if you reinstall ("Restore Purchases").

Other data

  • We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.
  • Android permissions used: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (ads and purchases), VIBRATE (haptics), and the Google Play billing permission.
  • The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of its own.

Children

The game is designed to be safe for children: no accounts, no chat, no personal data collection, no external links, and child-directed, G-rated, non-personalised ads only. Purchases require the Google Play purchase flow, where parental controls and authentication can be applied.

Data retention and your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data held by Google (AdMob, Google Play) use the controls in your Google Account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new "last updated" date.
