Parcel Panic: Sort & Deliver - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05. Developer: Ranveer Sidhu (ranveer.sidhu91@gmail.com).

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings and purchases-state are stored in a save file on your device. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Cloud save (optional)

If cloud save is enabled in your build, the game signs in anonymously with Firebase Authentication and stores your save (progress, coins, settings; never payment data) in Google Cloud Firestore under a random anonymous ID so it can follow you to a new phone. Crash reports (error message, game version, OS name) may be sent to the same service.

Analytics

We use GameAnalytics to count anonymous gameplay events (for example level started/completed, number of taps, time spent, ad shown, purchase made). This helps us find levels that are too hard. Events contain no personal information; GameAnalytics may process a device-generated identifier.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising ID to show and measure ads. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy & Consent. You can also reset or disable your advertising ID in your Android settings. Rewarded ads are always optional.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive a purchase token to confirm your purchase (verified through a Google Cloud Function against the Google Play Developer API); we never see your card details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and ad content is limited to G-rated by default.

Your choices

You can delete the game's data by uninstalling it. To ask for deletion of cloud data, email us with the install ID shown at the bottom of the Settings screen.
