Firefly Lights – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Firefly Lights is developed by Ranveer Singh Sidhu. The developer does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Game data

Level progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads via Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and usage data to serve and measure ads. Where required, consent is requested through Google's User Messaging Platform. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses ad data.

Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. The developer never receives your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
