Privacy Policy - Firefly Lights

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Firefly Lights ("the game") is a light-up logic puzzle game for Android intended for players aged 13 and over. This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Publisher: Ranveer Singh Sidhu - Contact: dev.ranveersinghsidhu@gmail.com - Policy URL: https://ranveersidhu.github.io/fireflylights/privacy.html

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. Your progress is stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which collects and uses some device data to serve ads. In regions where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time (menu > Privacy settings).
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13.

Information that stays on your device

Level progress, stars, whether the tutorial is done, daily-challenge and streak data, whether Remove Ads was purchased, and the time of your last free hint are saved in a local file in the game's private storage. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads (watch a short video to get a hint) and interstitial ads between levels. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may process your Android advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, device and app information, and ad interactions, and (where you consented, if required) use them to personalise ads. Ad content is limited to the "Teen" maximum rating. We do not receive this data. How Google uses it: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

  • Consent: where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK) the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) on first launch. You can reopen it from the menu under Privacy settings.
  • Ad personalisation: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Remove Ads removes the interstitial ads. Rewarded ads are always optional.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The one-time in-app purchase "Remove Ads" is processed by Google Play under Google's own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. The game only receives confirmation that a purchase succeeded (product id and a purchase token) so it can unlock the purchase and restore it if you reinstall.

Other data and permissions

  • We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.
  • Android permissions in the released app: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (ads and purchases), VIBRATE (haptics), WAKE_LOCK (background ad tasks), the Google Play billing permission, and the advertising-ID permission used by AdMob.
  • The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of its own.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and your rights

We hold no personal data about you on our systems, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data held by Google (AdMob, Google Play), use the controls in your Google Account: https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights of access, correction, deletion and objection regarding data processed by Google; Google is the controller for that data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new "last updated" date.
